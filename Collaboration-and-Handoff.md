# Collaboration and Handoff

## Programme boundaries

Jason Duran Dutton leads the SEFI–DEFI–GWFM programme's theoretical formulation, mathematical synthesis, computational model development, reproducibility records, and doctoral integration. Its constructions are hypotheses and research tools, not established physical premises.

Specialist collaborators would provide independent analysis, domain-specific modeling, benchmarking, and experimental assessment. DNA geometry is a handoff domain rather than a principal doctoral contribution. Biological interpretation and validation would be led by biophysics specialists; a golden-ratio or helical analogy does not establish a biological mechanism.

## Collaboration and handoff requirements

Each collaboration should define the model, assumptions, deliverables, evidence standard, reproducibility package, and respective contributions. Preserve existing baselines and unsuccessful results; version changes to governing equations or numerical methods.

DNA handoff should supply the existing manuscript, geometric definitions, available code and data, and an explicit list of untested biological claims. Scope, authorship, attribution, and responsibility for validation should be agreed with receiving specialists.

Cross-domain resemblance does not establish universal coupling, physical equivalence, or validation of the central field programme. Submission identifiers record provenance only and do not establish publication or acceptance.
